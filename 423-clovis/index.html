The complete Winter Inspection

Massachusetts winters are hard on a house. Once a year, before the cold sets in, we go through the points that cause the most expensive problems.

  • 🚪
    Doors and windowsA thorough check for gaps and drafts, for better heat retention and lower energy bills.
  • 🍂
    Gutter checkHelps prevent ice dams and water getting into the house during the thaw.
  • 🔧
    Garden hosesDisconnected and stored properly so outdoor faucets don't freeze.
  • ❄️
    Outdoor pipesExposed pipes protected and insulated against freezing.
  • 💧
    Water heater flushSediment removed from the tank, extending its life and efficiency.

What the membership does not cover

We prefer to be clear from day one. These situations belong to your Homeowner's Insurance.

Major structural damage

Serious damage to the foundation, structural walls, columns and main beams of the house.

Major floods and natural disasters

Large floods, severe storms, hurricanes or widespread water intrusion caused by major structural failures.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How long is the agreement?

12 months, billed monthly. It renews automatically unless either side gives notice at least 30 days before the end of the term.

How do the labor hours work?

Your plan includes 4, 8 or 12 labor hours per year. Each visit uses at least 1 hour, and the $70 call fee applies per visit. When the hours run out, extra labor is $100 per hour.

Are materials included?

No. Parts, paint, screws, pipes and other materials are billed separately, and only after you approve them.

When is the Winter Inspection done?

Once a year, before the harsh part of winter. We schedule it with you through your member area.

How do I pay?

By card through Stripe, a secure payment platform used by millions of businesses. You can update your card at any time from your account.

Which areas do you serve?

Worcester and the surrounding communities in Massachusetts. Not sure if your address is covered? Call or text us at (774) 462-9003.
